How to play

  • Mouse: click a difference on either picture. A red ring marks each find.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move the crosshair, Enter clicks, Tab swaps pictures.
  • Hint (H): pulses a ring around one unfound difference. Costs 50 points, 30s cooldown.
  • Lens (M): a 2x magnifier that follows your mouse over the pictures.
  • Scoring: +100 per find, combo bonus for quick finds in a row, −25 for a wrong click, time bonus at the win.
